Drop Rally – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Drop Rally mobile game (package com.granj215.droprally).

Summary

Drop Rally has an online leaderboard. When you set a record, the game creates an anonymous player for your install and sends your best times to our server. We do not ask for your name, email, account or any other personal information. The game shows optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob) and measures how it is played and when it crashes (Google Firebase). It does not contain in-app purchases.

Data we collect

The first time you finish a race and set a record, the game automatically creates an anonymous player on our server with:

  • a random player ID;
  • a player name made up by the game (for example "TurboFox 482"). You cannot type your own name;
  • a secret key that the game keeps on your device to prove the player is yours. We only store a scrambled (hashed) form of it;
  • the date the player was created.

When you beat one of your records, the game sends your race time, your fastest descent time and your top speed in that race. The top speed is only used to check that the times are possible and is not stored. We keep your best time of each kind and the date you set it.

When the game talks to our server, your IP address is used to limit how many players can be created in a short time (to stop abuse). It is not stored with your player. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs of requests (such as IP address and time) for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

Only to run the leaderboard: to show the best times and your place on it, and to stop cheating and abuse. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the provider that hosts our server.

Ads

After a race you can choose to watch a video ad to double the coins you won. Ads are never shown unless you ask for one. They are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners collect from your device:

  • the advertising ID of your device;
  • device information (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • your IP address, which gives your approximate location (country or city), not your exact one;
  • which ads you saw and how you interacted with them.

Google may use this data to show you personalised ads, and shares it with its advertising partners. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings > AD PRIVACY. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn off ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on Android).

Analytics and crash reports

The game uses Google Firebase to understand how it is played and to fix errors:

  • Firebase Analytics records events in the game: races started, finished and left, your place, time and falls, the frame rate, whether you watched an ad, the daily tasks you claimed and the colors you bought. It also records general information such as your device model, operating system version, language, country (from your IP address), the app version, and how often and how long you play. Events are linked to an app-instance ID created for your install and, on Android, to your advertising ID.
  • Firebase Crashlytics records, when the game crashes, what the game was doing (the error and its stack trace), your device model, operating system version and free memory, and an installation ID.

We use this data only in aggregate, to improve the game and fix errors. It is not linked to your leaderboard player. Google processes it on our behalf (see the Firebase privacy information). Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

What other players see

The leaderboard is public inside the game: other players can see your player name, your best times and your place. They cannot see your player ID or anything else.

Where it is stored

Our server and database run on Cloudflare (Cloudflare Workers and D1), and the ads, analytics and crash reports are handled by Google. Both may process data in different countries, including the United States. Data is sent over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).

How long we keep it

Your player and your times are kept while the leaderboard exists. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the secret key on your device is lost and the game creates a new player, but the old player stays on the leaderboard until you ask us to delete it.

Deleting your data

To have your player and your times deleted, email us at the address below with your player name (shown in the Ranking tab) and, if you can, one of your times. We will delete it within 30 days. Analytics data is not linked to your name or your player: to stop it being linked to your device, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings; uninstalling the game removes its app-instance ID.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (coins, colors, daily tasks, records and settings) and your player's secret key are saved on your device. Your coins, colors and settings are never sent to us (only the events listed under "Analytics and crash reports"), and all of it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game uses the internet for the leaderboard, the ads, the analytics and the crash reports, and the advertising ID for the ads and the analytics. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13. It does not ask for any personal information from anyone. Player names are made up by the game, so no one can write their real name or a message on the leaderboard.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated on this page before that version is released, with a new date at the top.
