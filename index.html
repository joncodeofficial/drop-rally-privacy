Drop Rally – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Drop Rally mobile game (package com.granj215.droprally).

Data we collect

Drop Rally does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game does not use analytics, advertising or tracking services, and it does not contain in-app purchases.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (such as your current level) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

The game does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the game changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated on this page.
